# Privacy Policy for TaskIyagi

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## Overview
TaskIyagi is a taskbar and panel for Linux desktops.

## Data Collection
TaskIyagi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. Settings and sticky notes stay on your own PC. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Network Use
TaskIyagi does not need an internet connection and does not send any data to external servers.
